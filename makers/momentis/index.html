MOMENTIS is a new independent watch brand from Delémont, in the Swiss Jura, and its first watch, the MW-001, was unveiled on 11 November 2026 after six years of development. The website is published by Veltrion SA and refers to its founders without naming them, and it gives no founding date, so this listing does not either. The brand frames its watchmaking as the patient work of many people, rooted in a region where, it says, mechanics is a craft passed down by hand, and describes time as something to question and understand but never fully master.

The MW-001 is a hand-wound tourbillon with a single barrel, a 102-hour power reserve, and a 4 Hz (28,800 vph) beat with 20 jewels. The 16.2 mm tourbillon cage sits at 6 o'clock and turns once a minute. The openworked main plate and five bridges are brass, designed in-house, with a satin anthracite NAC coating, polished bevels and hand decoration, and the gear train, wheels, pinions and barrel are rhodium-plated and polished. The watch shows hours and minutes only, on black satin and polished hands over a 0.55 mm sapphire flange in a transparent brown metallised finish made in Switzerland.

The case is grade 5 titanium, 39 mm across and 10.4 mm thick including the crystals, with a polished bezel and case back, a radially brushed case middle and a domed sapphire crystal, and it weighs 29 g without the strap. It is water resistant to 5 ATM. Two lug styles are offered: signature lugs, four separate titanium-wire lugs screwed to the case middle, or shorter, more steeply curved lugs that fit under a cuff. The crown is set with a tiger's-eye disc behind a three-armed motif, which the brand calls its signature feature, and the sapphire case back is engraved 'Prototype', 'Jura-Suisse', 'Tourbillon' and 'MOMENTIS'. The strap is Swiss-made alligator or smooth calfskin on an 18 mm titanium pin buckle, and the watch comes in a walnut box with a numbered aluminium certificate and a five-year warranty.

The first run is deliberately small. The Founders Edition is nine numbered pieces, with the tiger's-eye crown and a brown flange, at CHF 111,000 excluding VAT, and it was shown privately to select collectors in early November 2026. A pre-series of six pieces has a transparent flange and no tiger's-eye crown, and its price is not listed. The case, movement and finishing are the same for both. The brand plans three series of eleven pieces from 2027 and has a GMT version in development. The watch is designed in Delémont and made, assembled and regulated in Switzerland, and buyers are asked to request a meeting with a founder before allocation, when the lug style is chosen.

MOMENTIS is a new name, so there is little track record to report: the website names no founders or watchmakers, lists no press coverage, and does not say who makes the movement beyond the in-house plate and bridge design and 'made in Switzerland'. We have therefore not described the calibre as in-house or manufactured by the brand, and the details above come from the brand's own site and press release.
