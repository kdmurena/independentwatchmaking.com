Zeroo Time is a Japanese watch brand that presents itself as Tokyo-based, with watches that it says are crafted and assembled by hand in Japan. According to the brand's About page it was founded in 2017 by a designer who goes by the name Syuu and who drew on classic watch designs. The brand says it wants to rethink the traditional wristwatch through new design and technology, and argues that a watch is now an expression of identity rather than only a way to tell the time. It aims to offer luxury at affordable prices, and the pages we read do not name factories or production sites.

The T Series is made up of skeletonised tourbillon watches in 316L stainless steel cases with sapphire crystals and 30 m water resistance, on a fluorine rubber or leather strap or a steel bracelet depending on the model. The T1 The Archer is a 43 mm manually wound flying tourbillon with a 60-hour power reserve at EUR 3,200. The T4-01 The Archer is a 41 mm by 48 mm tonneau with a bridgeless flying tourbillon in a spiral silver carriage (calibre ZT01A, 60 hours), limited to 1,000 pieces per colour at EUR 3,400, as is its T4-01 Tri-Edge Limited Edition (EUR 3,420, sold out). The T3 The Leo, the brand's first automatic skeleton tourbillon (calibre ZT03, 43 mm, about 72 hours, EUR 3,220), is sold out. The T5 (42 mm) and T7 (46 mm) The Archer, at EUR 3,800, have manually wound twin-barrel tourbillons with 20 jewels, and the T7 is limited to 1,000 pieces per colour. The T8 The Orion (EUR 5,300) is the brand's first octagonal watch, 43 mm, with calibre ZT08, a spiral flying tourbillon and twin barrels, also limited to 1,000 pieces per colour. The T9 and T9-01 The Ufo are automatic tourbillons (calibre ZT09, double barrel, free-sprung escapement, 60 hours, 35 jewels) in 46 mm by 40 mm tonneau cases at EUR 3,400. The T10 The Archer, from EUR 3,500, is a 51 mm by 43 mm automatic with a flying tourbillon at nine o'clock, a gunmetal rotor, 60 hours and 35 jewels. All of the brand's pages state that the watches are crafted in Tokyo, Japan.

The DT1 The Gemini, the only model in the DT Series, has a hand-wound double tourbillon (calibre ZDT01, 21 jewels, 42 hours) in a 45 mm three-layer octagonal case rated to 50 m, and is listed at EUR 4,250. It is sold out and described as made to order. The M Series is the brand's sports-luxury line. The M2 The Subaru, a 39 mm by 50 mm tonneau limited to 2,999 pieces and now discontinued, was the first automatic in the series and the brand's first collaboration with a Swiss movement maker, using the Swiss automatic calibre ZM02 (26 jewels, 40 hours). The M3 The Lyra is a 41 mm octagonal, 9.7 mm thick, with the same ZM02 calibre, 50 m water resistance and a steel bracelet, at EUR 1,930 for the Classic and EUR 2,470 for the Skeleton. The M6 The UFO is a hand-wound skeleton watch (calibre ZM06-M, 16 jewels, 40 hours, 37.5 mm by 40.9 mm) at about EUR 1,117, shown as a pre-order with first shipments planned for December 2026. The C5 The Keigo, limited to 300 pieces at EUR 1,000, is a 38 mm watch with a microrotor automatic calibre 50ZC02 (42 hours) and a day/night indicator, moon phase, small seconds and date. The Farewell Series, described as the final run of the brand's iconic watches, made in limited quantities and not restocked once sold out, includes the T1, DT1, T3 and T5.

The Dutch website, Zeroo Time the Netherlands, is presented as the official European dealer and distributor. The brand expanded distribution to Europe in 2022, selling its full range online and through select dealers. The site offers insured worldwide shipping, a 24-month warranty and a maintenance service.
