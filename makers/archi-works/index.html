Archi Works is a Paris-based watch brand that calls itself a manufacture intemporelle and a confidential independent microbrand, founded in 2022. It was founded by Richard D. Ménasé, who is also the publication director and owner of Archi Group, and who dates his horology passion and industry experience to 2015. The watches are designed in Paris and made in Switzerland, combining Swiss watchmaking with Parisian design sensibility. Archiwatch, at archiwatch.com, is the brand's online store, and many earlier releases, such as the Anonymous and Original models, were sold under the Archiwatch name. Orders are handled as pre-orders that run for 10 to 20 business days before production starts, and limited editions are final sale.

The brand's early releases were homages to the Patek Philippe Calatrava ref. 96, in a 36 mm steel case rather than the original's 31 mm, styled as new old stock pieces that look as if they had never been sold or worn. After the Merci x The Archiwatch LMM-01, an Italian-inspired edition of 250 pieces in 2022, came the 2023 Anonymous collection, ref. 2510, which has no brand on the dial in a nod to unbranded school-certificate watches, and the 2525 Original and Explorer models. These use a modified Sellita SW210 hand-wound movement with a 42-hour power reserve in a 316L steel case 8.5 mm thick, with 20 mm lugs, 5 ATM of water resistance, a Molequin leather strap and a Forstner beads-of-rice bracelet. The 2024 releases included the Tokyo and Osaka editions, limited to 10 pieces each, and a gold and steel Original limited to 25 pieces that the brand called the final collection of its Anonymous era. Prices were EUR 1,690 to 2,000, and the edition sizes on the product pages are not always consistent with the collection descriptions.

The Padellone, reference 55, is an homage to the Patek Philippe 3448, produced from 1962 to 1981 and described by the brand as the world's first series-produced self-winding perpetual calendar wristwatch. It is built on a Dubois Dépraz DD90313 automatic calendar module, which shows the day, date and moon phase and is typically paired with an ETA 2892-A2 or Sellita SW300 base, with a 42-hour power reserve at 28,800 vibrations per hour. The 316L steel case is 37.75 mm across and 11.4 mm thick with 20 mm lugs and 3 ATM of water resistance, and the dial has day and month apertures, a moon phase and date subdial at six o'clock and a painted minute track in place of the pearls of earlier versions. A 2025 Panna dial was followed in 2026 by a black dial and four Archi Works panna dials named Moonlight, Azure, Aurora and Sunset, at EUR 2,500 for the earlier editions and EUR 3,000 for the 2026 Archi Works versions, all now sold out. A one-off 18k yellow gold prototype was shown as not for sale and will not be produced.

The Diplomat is a 2026 collaboration with Amsterdam Vintage Watches, in Azure Silver Sector and Aurora Cream Sector dials priced at EUR 2,250 excluding taxes. The 35 mm by 8.65 mm steel case has a sapphire exhibition back, and the dial pairs faceted sapphire or emerald baguette markers with diamond hour markers, a recessed small seconds and a double signature. It uses a hand-wound La Joux-Perret Peseux 7001 with Soigné finishing, a 50-hour power reserve and 5 ATM of water resistance, on a Molequin Saffiano strap. The page calls it a limited edition of 100 timepieces, but its specifications list serial numbers from 1 to 200.

The brand's pages give a 90-day guarantee with a one-year mechanical guarantee after service, though some specification lists say one year outright, and withdrawal rights do not apply to limited editions. The brand's legal text is operated by Archi Group SARL, and its terms and shipping text refer in places to Harbor Watches, which looks like copied template text. Hodinkee has covered the Classic Two-Tone and the LMM-01.
