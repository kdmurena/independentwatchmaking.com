Marathon Watch Company is a family-run maker of military-style watches and timing instruments, with the tagline 'Best in the Long Run'. The brand says its watches are designed in Canada and handcrafted in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, and describes them as purpose-built to full military specifications. The business is run by the fourth generation of the Wein family, with Mitchell Wein as president and Leon Wein as a former president. The brand's website does not describe the company's ownership structure beyond the family's involvement.

According to the company's About page, the family business began in 1904 as Weinstrum Watch (spelled 'Weinsturm' elsewhere on the same page), later renamed Wein Brothers. Morris Wein founded Marathon Watch in 1939 and supplied precision timepieces across North America. The brand says it has manufactured timing instruments for Allied forces since 1941, and markets its military-style products as 'Authentic Military Heritage with Modern Military Design'. The site does not name specific military contracts.

The watch collection is organised by role. The dive watches, called SAR (Search and Rescue), come in 36 mm, 41 mm and 46 mm cases and include the automatic GSAR, the quartz TSAR and JSAR, the automatic OSAR-D, the Jumbo Day/Date and the 46 mm CSAR automatic chronograph. A 41 mm titanium GSAR is the newest release. The pilot's watches are the 41 mm Navigator range (SSNAV, SSNAV-D and the CeraShell NAV), in quartz or automatic. The General Purpose line (GPM, GPQ) has 34 mm, 36 mm and 41 mm mechanical and quartz watches in steel or composite Fibreshell cases. Many models carry military 'Standard-Issue' and Type I or Type II labels and NATO stock numbers in the 6645 class, which the site does not explain, and some listings refer to tritium or MaraGlo luminous material.

The special projects collection holds limited editions and collaborations, including the Arctic Edition, Red Maple and Grey Maple, Anthracite, USMC, IDF Duvdevan, YAMAM and Jeep Series (Rubicon and Willys) editions, and a limited-edition collection that includes the ADANAC Navigator, Blue Yonder and US 250 Anthracite. Marathon also sells mechanical and digital stopwatches, wall and desk clocks, weather instruments, tools and tactical gear, plus straps and bracelets, servicing, and engraving. New watches carry a three-year warranty when registered. The brand's pages do not state the movements used in its watches or the production volumes. Prices on the website currently range from about USD 315 to USD 5,900 for watches, with many items shown at sale prices.
