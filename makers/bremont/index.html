Bremont Watch Company operates from The Wing in Henley-on-Thames, Oxfordshire — the first industrial watchmaking facility built in Great Britain for generations, and the site of what Bremont describes as the only genuine end-to-end watch manufacture in the UK. Set in the wooded hills and rolling fields of the Chilterns, alongside a river town tied to English tradition through nearly two centuries of the Henley Royal Regatta, Bremont's stated spirit is drawn directly from that landscape and its history of British adventurers and explorers: the same instinct for precision timekeeping that produced John Harrison's revolutionary marine chronometers in the 17th and 18th centuries, born of the practical need to know where you are and what time it is when far out in the unexplored corners of the world. At The Wing, Bremont masters the entire watchmaking process in-house — design, development, prototyping, assembly, testing, and servicing — a level of vertical integration highly unusual among British watch brands, most of which assemble from externally sourced components rather than manufacture at this depth.

That manufacturing philosophy is organised around six stated principles. Proudly British anchors everything to The Wing and its end-to-end capability. Robustness reflects Bremont's evolution from hardened 316L to 904L stainless steel — an alloy developed for aerospace and marine applications, chosen for superior corrosion resistance, scratch resistance, and a distinctive lustre unavailable from more conventional watch steels. Mechanical Only commits the brand exclusively to high-grade Swiss mechanical movements, sourced for precision and reliability regardless of the environment a Bremont watch is asked to endure. Visibility governs dial design, with the Terra Nova range in particular using the latest generation of Super-LumiNova and full-block luminous indexes for readability in the most demanding conditions. Comfort extends the same engineering rigour to every case, strap, and bracelet, each designed for ergonomic fit as much as durability. And Craftsmanship underpins the hand-finishing and intricate assembly applied throughout — including Bremont's NATO straps, produced using traditional looms dating to the 1800s and more than two centuries of accumulated textile expertise. Nearly two decades of aviation watchmaking sit at the brand's foundation, with Bremont instruments deployed by military divisions and enthusiast pilots alike, engineered specifically to endure wherever they're taken.

The current collection is organised by function across three core families. Altitude is Bremont's pilot watch line, including the MBIII and the Meteorite-dial MB Meteor references (Time Zones, 'Felix the Cat,' and Stealth variants), alongside a dedicated Air Force Blue capsule collection. Supermarine is the dive watch range, rated to 300 metres and built for underwater performance without compromising the brand's design language. Terra Nova is the field watch family, encompassing everyday and date references, the Terra Nova HRR Chronograph tied to the Henley Royal Regatta, and — marking 250 years of America — the Terra Nova 1776 Jumping Hour, a genuine jumping-hour complication developed as a dedicated commemorative piece. Bremont's His Majesty's Armed Forces (HMAF) Collection extends across Altitude, Supermarine, and Terra Nova cases alike, produced in direct support of British military charities through the brand's Armed Forces Subsidy Scheme, while Bremont's Military Projects and Corporate & Special Projects divisions undertake bespoke commissions for defence and commercial clients beyond the standing collection. The brand's first tourbillon, documented in its own Crafted journal series, marked a significant recent step into higher complications, and Bremont continues to expand into new territory with initiatives like the Bremont Moon Mission I and a growing roster of ambassadors including adventurer, photographer, and filmmaker Jimmy Chin. Every Bremont watch is hand-built in the UK, ships with free delivery and up to a 5-year extended warranty, and is backed by 0% finance options through the brand's own website.
