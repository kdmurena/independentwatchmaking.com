Mark & Mors is an Italian watch brand whose homepage promises a new era of Italian watchmaking. The brand page says it was founded by three people with complementary skills, covering project vision, hands-on experience in mechanical watchmaking and high-level automotive design, and names their surnames as Marchesini, Moretto and Marchiori without assigning roles. The designer is a partner in the brand and comes from Italy's Emilia Motor Valley, with more than ten years of car design experience. The idea began on an evening in May 2024, when the founders sat down with a blank sheet of paper and a pencil. The brand does not publish a headquarters or production address, saying only that the founders come from an area tied to the sea and unspoiled landscapes, so the listing gives Italy without a city.

The brand's philosophy treats time as a reflection of how people choose to live, with family, relationships and meaningful moments giving everyday life its significance, and the closing line of its brand page translates as time passes, but what we live remains. Its logo is three shields converging toward the centre, standing for the founders' shared direction and stylising their surname initials; Mainspring describes the symbols as leaf-shaped Ms. The watches use automatic mechanical movements, favour bold lines and balanced proportions, and are meant to keep their identity rather than follow trends. Each collection is produced in limited quantities.

The first collection, Opera Viva, is a dive watch designed entirely in Italy and named for the submerged part of a ship's hull. It comes in three variants: Night Sea, Red Dawn, with a bordeaux and champagne dial, and Blue Wake, with a deep blue dial. All share a 39 mm by 12.4 mm case with a 48 mm lug-to-lug in 316L steel with a sapphire crystal, a screw-down crown at four o'clock, a screw-down caseback and 300 m of water resistance. The unidirectional 120-click ceramic bezel has a backlit 0 to 20 scale, derived from the roughly 20 minutes of oxygen in a diver's tank. The movement is a Sellita SW200-1 Elaboré, hand-regulated in three positions, with a 38-hour power reserve; Mainspring reports it is factory-regulated to within about ±7 seconds per day, up to ±20. Mainspring says the Opera Viva was born in the Po Delta in north-eastern Italy, which inspired the bezel's zero index. The watch is described as hand-assembled and regulated in Switzerland with Swiss Made certification, on a solid-link bracelet that tapers from 20 mm to 16 mm, with a tropic-style FKM rubber strap included.

The case is named Anima, after the Latin word for soul, and the brand says the design is proprietary, with a patent filing. Each Opera Viva model is priced at EUR 1,250, with Blue Wake shown reduced to EUR 875. The product pages state a limited edition of 100 pieces, but the Night Sea page also carries a banner calling it permanently sold out, the Red Dawn page lists all 100 as available, and the collection page shows Night Sea and Red Dawn as sold out, so availability on the brand's pages is inconsistent. All purchases carry a 24-month international warranty on the mechanical parts, free returns within 14 days and a try-at-home option.

The second line, Altovento Riviera, is a slimmer dress-sport watch in two colourways, Dolomia, inspired by the pink Enrosadira glow on the Dolomite peaks, and Riva, inspired by the Riviera del Conero. It has a 38 mm case, 8.5 mm thick, in 316L steel, with a domed box-coated sapphire crystal, a coined screw-down caseback, 100 m of water resistance, a Miyota 9039 movement and an Italian leather strap. The brand's page gives a reference price of EUR 730 and a launch price of EUR 399 for a 48-hour window starting on 1 December 2026, and says the series is strictly limited without stating a number. The page carries a Kickstarter logo, but does not say how the launch is tied to Kickstarter.
