Panzera was founded in 2009 in Sydney by Andrew Herman and Roger Cooper, built around a direct-to-consumer model that removes conventional retail overhead to put genuinely well-made timepieces within reach of a wider audience. The brand began with a single Flieger pilot's watch and has since shipped more than 300,000 timepieces to customers in over 100 countries. Three 'worlds of exploration' guide the collections that followed — AIR for pilot's watches, LAND for motorsport-inspired pieces, and SEA for marine and dive watches — echoed directly in Panzera's star logo, styled as a navigational compass. Watches are designed in Australia and hand-assembled across two dedicated workshops, one in Sydney and one in the Ticino canton of Switzerland, using components sourced from Germany, Switzerland, Japan, and Italy. The dedicated Swiss Made line is assembled in Switzerland using genuine Swiss movements and carries official Swiss Made certification; every watch, regardless of collection, undergoes a minimum of five days of quality testing and is laser-engraved with its own unique serial number, backed by a two-year international warranty.

The brand's own flagship is the Aquamarine 45C, a Swiss-made automatic chronograph built around the Valjoux 7750 — 25 jewels, 28,800vph, 41 hours of reserve, with day, date, and chronograph functions and hacking seconds, the same movement family that has powered watches from Rolex, Omega, Breitling, and IWC over the decades, albeit finished to a different standard here. Its 45mm, 316L stainless steel case carries a brushed decagonal bezel, screw-down crown, and exhibition display back rated to 200 metres, offered in matte black, midnight blue, or pure white dials on a quick-release steel bracelet, leather, or rubber strap, priced from roughly 5,790 to 6,175 Australian dollars depending on configuration. Beyond that Swiss-made flagship line, Panzera's broader catalogue spans Classic dress watches, Pilot chronographs, Sports models, and a dedicated Aquamarine dive-watch family at generally more accessible price points, alongside a Women's line and a custom-design programme for bespoke commissions; every order ships worldwide direct from the brand's Sydney warehouse, with all duties and taxes included at checkout and a 30-day, no-questions-asked return policy.
