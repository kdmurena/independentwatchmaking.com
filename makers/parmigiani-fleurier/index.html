Founded on May 29, 1996 at the Grand Salon Sandoz of the Beau-Rivage Palace in Lausanne, Parmigiani Fleurier was born from a lifelong devotion to restoration and the mechanical arts. Michel Parmigiani — Prix Gaïa laureate 1995 — trained as a master restorer, spending decades preserving exceptional horological masterpieces for the Sandoz Family Foundation.

His philosophy: 'Restoration taught me that creation begins with understanding what came before. By reviving the past, you learn to build the future with coherence and gratitude. Perfection is not an end; it's a way of being.' His lifelong passion for nature's perfection and the Golden Ratio profoundly shaped his designs. The Maison launched in 1996 with 52 references — from elegant simplicity to haute complications. Supported by the Sandoz Foundation, Parmigiani Fleurier operates a fully integrated manufacture in Val de Travers, where nearly 350 artisans craft every component in-house. Key milestones: 1996 — Toric, the first watch designed by Michel Parmigiani (knurled bezel and fluted details evoking Greek columns); 1999 — Ionica, first wristwatch with an 8-day power reserve (PF110, a world first); 2004 — Bugatti Type 370, a world first with tubular movement along a single axis like a car engine; 2009 — Fibonacci Pocket Watch, embodying the Golden Ratio; 2010 — Ultra-flat Tonda 1950 with first micro-rotor movement; 2016 — Equus Forma Mechanica automaton hippologia; 2016 — Chronor split-second chronograph for the 20th anniversary (integrated gold movement); 2017 — Toric Hémisphère, world premiere dual-timezone calibre; 2019 — Hijri Perpetual Calendar wristwatch, world premiere; 2021 — Tonda PF Micro-Rotor, the 'rebirth milestone'; 2022 — Tonda PF GMT Rattrapante, world premiere; 2023 — Tonda PF Minute Rattrapante, world premiere; 2023 — Cultural Calendars (Xiali Complete Calendar, world premiere); 2024 — Toric return with all-gold manual movement; 2024 — L'Armoriale Pocket Watch Objet d'Art; 2025 — Ultra-Cermet Sport Chronograph, world first ceramic/metal fusion across all components; 2025 — Toric Quantième Perpétuel with PF733 calibre (5.15mm thick, solid rose gold).

Current collections: Tonda PF, Toric, Tonda PF Sport, Objets d'Art. 2026 marks thirty years of independent watchmaking.
