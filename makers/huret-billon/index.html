Huret Billon is a French independent watchmaking duo formed by Tanguy Huret and Pierre Billon, based in Morteau in the French Jura. The company was registered in Morteau at the end of August 2025, with Huret as president and Billon as managing director, and its registered activity covers the design, transformation, repair and restoration of mechanical and electronic watches, the trade of horological products, and consulting, training and research and development. Both founders' individual work predates the company, and each has his own listing in this catalogue.

Tanguy Huret came to horology after starting with a computer science degree. He won the F.A. Lange Excellence Prize in 2016 with his Full Calendar and went on to the horology diploma course at the Lycée Edgar Faure in Morteau. Pierre Billon trained at the same school, where he made the graduation watch Tarasia in 2017.

The pair describe their approach as traditional artisanal watchmaking and hand-finishing, including hand-beveled edges, black (blocked) polishing, flame-blued parts and custom component work, alongside unique and prototype pieces. On Instagram they say that a few years ago they set themselves the challenge of designing and crafting a watch entirely on their own, to deepen their understanding of artisanal watchmaking, and that the piece embodies their shared vision of craftsmanship. A prototype, shown as a pièce unique, has a subdial at six o'clock with heat-blued Clous de Paris guilloche.

Huret Billon does not appear to have an official website, and we found no published prices, model names, movement details or technical specifications, so this listing is limited to what the founders have shared on Instagram and what the company register shows. The details above come from the founders' own Instagram posts and from the public company register, and we have not seen the watches in person.
