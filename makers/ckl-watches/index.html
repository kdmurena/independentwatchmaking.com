CKL Watches is an independent Taiwanese watch brand founded in 2019 by Che-Kuan Liao (廖哲寬), a classically trained watchmaker and master repairer who restored hundreds of watches before starting his own brand. The brand's philosophy is artisanal craftsmanship, mechanical innovation and original design. It combines traditional watchmaking skills with creative engineering and offers a high degree of personalisation, and each watch is said to be assembled and finished by the founder, with many components modified by hand or made to order. The brand is respected by independent and microbrand collectors, particularly in Taiwan, and is gaining international recognition. The brand's website is the shop of the Taipei retailer Link & Tag, which says that CKL is one of the two creative arms under its LT Project Watches umbrella and that CKL focuses on mechanical watchmaking, including movement modification, technical refinement and structural integrity.

The watches are built on standard movements that CKL heavily modifies. Model 1 Gen 2, the Floating Balance Wheel, is a hand-wound watch based on the ETA 6497 (18 jewels, about 46 hours of power reserve), in which the balance wheel floats above the dial on a bridge and is visible from the front, in a 44 mm case with a sapphire crystal and a dial in black, green or grey. Model 2, 'Reversed Movement, Dial-Side Rotor', puts the winding rotor on the dial side, in a 44 mm stainless steel case that is 10.6 mm thick, inspired by the M.A.D.1 but built on CKL's own engineering; the article reports that it is limited to 99 pieces, offered with Miyota, Seagull or Unitas movements, and made in collaboration with Fiber Watch for the case and strap and with Link & Tag for design and marketing. The Model 2 Centre, a 2025 novelty, reworks the Unitas 6497 into an inverted calibre with Geneva stripes and CKL's modular components on the dial side, in a 42.5 mm 316L stainless steel case, 12 mm thick, with 50 m water resistance and a sapphire caseback.

Model 3, the 'Three Bridges Regulator', is a skeletonised hand-wound regulator watch in a 43 mm 316L stainless steel case that takes the three-bridge layout associated with Girard-Perregaux and has no logo. Its order book is full and orders are closed. Model V, the 'Inverted Escapement System', has a large, centrally mounted floating balance wheel held by a V-shaped bridge, with the hours and minutes on subdials around it and a small seconds subdial. It uses a heavily modified Unitas 6498 and is a 43 mm 316L stainless steel watch, 14.5 mm thick, with a double-domed sapphire crystal, a display caseback, 30 m water resistance and a 22 mm leather strap, in black, gold, green, grey or silver dials.

On the Link & Tag shop, Model 2 is listed at NT$33,600 and is out of stock, Model 1 Gen 2 is NT$74,995, the Model 2 Centre NT$89,995 and Model V NT$119,995. The Model 1 Gen 2 is made to order with delivery of about two months, and the Model 2 Centre and Model V are shown as pre-orders without payment or delivery terms. The article describes the Model 2 as costing about NT$49,800 to NT$55,000, which differs from the shop price, and the reviews mentioned in the article note that the Model 2's rotor can be loud and that the watch can rock on the wrist. The sources do not give annual production figures.
