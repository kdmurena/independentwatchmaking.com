OVD

OVD is a Hong Kong microbrand whose name stands for Origin, Vision & Design — a philosophy the brand describes as a deliberate return to the simplest foundation, to origin elements, with the pursuit of making beautiful objects that accurately serve their function standing as the creative impulse behind every new OVD design. The brand positions itself around distinctiveness above all else, aiming for pieces striking enough that, in its own words, someone will always ask 'hey, what kind of watch are you wearing?' — unique character taking precedence over convention. That ambition plays out through an unusually large and thematically eclectic catalogue for a brand of its size, with individual references drawing inspiration from a wide range of historical, scientific, and cultural touchstones rather than settling into a single signature design language.

The OVD Collection spans references including the RONIN and RONIN II, the F/01 V2, Moon Infinity, NASA Full Moon, NavAir, NASA Apollo, SATURNIAN, TITANICX REBORN and TITANICX-II, DAVINCI REBORN, BLUE MOON, MARS MISSION, OBJECT1, and the CHAKA collection — names that signal a design catalogue moving freely between space exploration, aviation, maritime history, and Renaissance-era invention as recurring visual and conceptual themes. The separate UW-Collection (underwater) applies the same eclectic approach to dive watches, spanning the Vortex, SharkX, AEQUOREA II, SealDiver, KRAKENER, SWORDFISHER, and WHALE DIVER references. Selected models, including the TITANICX REBORN, are built around Swiss Sellita movements finished with Super-LumiNova lume application, offered on both bracelet and leather strap configurations, with pricing for current references generally starting around USD 449. OVD sells directly through its own webshop, summarising its guiding motto in three words: 'Time for Innovation.'

Behind the webshop sits OD Studio, and the brand now operates a dual base spanning Japan and Hong Kong, with its Instagram describing itself simply as an invitation to 'join the OVD Universe.' Rather than relying solely on direct sales, OVD has increasingly turned to crowdfunding to launch individual references: the F/01 V2, a 1970s-racing-inspired chronograph-styled piece powered by the Miyota 9122 automatic movement, ran a Kickstarter campaign in October 2025 that closed with 182 backers pledging over HK$777,000 against a modest HK$50,000 goal — more than fifteen times its target. That pattern of small, focused capital raises for individual model lines, rather than one continuous factory-style release schedule, fits the brand's wider identity as a design-first microbrand assembling a sprawling, theme-driven catalogue one Kickstarter and one collection at a time, rather than settling into the singular signature aesthetic that defines most independents on this list. For a brand whose founding philosophy is a deliberate 'return to the simplest foundation,' OVD's growth path has been anything but simple — but it has proven remarkably effective at building a loyal, engaged following willing to fund each new theme before it ever reaches the shelf.
