Strom is a genuine three-generation family watchmaking house based in Biel/Bienne, Switzerland, built around the singular vision of patriarch Daniel Strom — described on the brand's own materials, without much exaggeration, as something close to a legendary creature in watchmaking, a master of every intricacy of the craft. For Daniel Strom, watches have never been simply technical marvels or status symbols; his stated philosophy is that a watch should reflect who a person truly is, not merely what they have achieved — an expression of heritage and personal aesthetics that makes each piece genuinely unique rather than a repeated commercial product. The company describes itself as a family, not a brand run by committees: every decision passes through one family, in one building, in Biel/Bienne. Today the entire Strom family is actively engaged in the business — Daniel's wife Rosanna Strom oversees accounting, son Loris Strom leads product development and manufacturing, and daughter Alexia Strom manages marketing and social media — with Loris and Alexia, as third-generation descendants of the Strom watchmaking name, actively carrying the family legacy forward. Every timepiece is assembled entirely in the family's own Biel/Bienne workshop and released only after passing the full run of mechanical and quality testing, backed by a two-year guarantee covering all watch functions and manufacturing integrity; every piece can also be personalised to an owner's wishes through direct contact with the atelier.

Agonium is Strom's haute horlogerie collection and the truest expression of Daniel Strom's personal artistic vision — a line he dreamed of for years before discovering the right alchemy of watchmaking and silversmithing needed to bring it fully to life. The result, in the brand's own words, is less a conventional timepiece than a sculpture: a wearable work of art built around existential questions of existence, mortality, and the fleeting nature of time, with the explicit intention not to control time but to remind the wearer to live it fully. The collection's family lines carry that same weight of meaning throughout: Memento Mori, Carpe Diem — Agonium's largest sub-collection at 21 models — confronts mortality directly through its very name; Nethuns II (13 models, including a Tourbillon Gold reference and a Tourbillon Corinthium Aes reference at CHF 69,000) draws on Neptune, the Etruscan-Roman god of the sea; Draco (9 models) and Archangeli (5 models) each build a distinct mythological or celestial visual language into their sculptural cases and dials; Michaeli (4 models) and Ventus 3 (5 models) continue the line's exploration of elemental and archangelic themes; and a striking one-off, In Memoriam H.R. Giger, pays tribute to the Swiss surrealist artist behind the design of Alien — a fittingly biomechanical, existential subject for the Agonium philosophy. Handcrafted sculpture, mechanical precision, and genuine silversmithing techniques run through the entire Agonium range, offered in materials spanning 18K red gold skeleton construction to sterling silver 925, most references available upon request given the bespoke, small-batch nature of the work.

Where Agonium is Daniel's philosophical statement, the CruiZer collection represents the vision of the third generation: led by Loris Strom, son of Daniel and the family's product development and manufacturing lead, CruiZer is a contemporary, motorsport-inspired line built around a distinctive cushion-shaped case — neither round nor square — defined by an innovative container-based case construction that gives the collection its own visual identity within the Strom range. Described by the brand as a fusion of sporty energy and lifestyle elegance, CruiZer spans an extensive range of sub-collections and price points: the accessible Evo CruiZer (8 models, from CHF 2,490); the Mech Automatic and Mech Chrono Big CruiZer lines; the Stealth Big CruiZer; dedicated Lady Sport and Lady Elegance CruiZer lines; Jewellery CruiZer and Jewellery Big CruiZer references set with precious stones; a Diamonds CruiZer line; a Classic CruiZer line; and rotating Special Editions CruiZer releases — altogether a considerably more accessible and production-scaled counterpart to Agonium's one-of-a-kind sculptural ambition, while remaining entirely assembled in the same family workshop under the same two-year guarantee.
