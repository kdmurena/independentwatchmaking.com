SpaceOne Watches Paris is a collaboration between Théo Auffret — the celebrated Parisian independent watchmaker — and Guillaume Laidet, watch entrepreneur. Designed in Paris and assembled in France, every component is manufactured in Switzerland by leading suppliers. The design is inspired by space exploration and the 'space-age' aesthetic that emerged during the original space race of the 1960s.

Three collections: Worldtimer, Tellurium, and the debut Jumping Hour. The Jumping Hour function was developed entirely by Théo Auffret's workshop and comprises nine bespoke parts.

The watch features a Swiss automatic P024 Soprod movement (no date), self-winding, 28,800vph (4Hz), 38-hour power reserve. Case: 42×51.67mm, 12.6mm thick, 22mm lug width. The sapphire-disc minute display gives maximum transparency; cockpit sapphire crystal; stainless steel see-through caseback. Available in stainless steel (90g), titanium (60g), and forged carbon (30g) — each with black/orange FKM rubber strap, 3 ATM water resistance. Two-year warranty.
